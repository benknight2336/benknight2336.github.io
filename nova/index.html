Nova

A personal desktop assistant that runs on your own computer.

Mail and calendars in one place

Nova connects to your own Gmail, iCloud, and Outlook accounts so you can read, sort, and send mail and see all your calendars together.

Local AI

Nova's assistant runs on your computer with a local model. Summaries, sorting, and suggested replies are made on your machine, not on a server.

Private by design

Nova has no servers and no accounts of its own. Your mail, calendar, and sign-in tokens stay on your computer.

Nova is a personal project and is not offered to the public.
